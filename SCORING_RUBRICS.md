# Speaking Scoring Rubrics

## Official Sources

- IELTS scoring detail: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail
- IELTS resources for setting scores: https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/understanding-ielts-scoring/resources-for-setting-your-ielts-scores
- IELTS Speaking key assessment criteria PDF: https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-key-assessment-criteria.pdf
- ETS TOEFL iBT Speaking section: https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content/speaking.html
- ETS Speaking Scoring Guide PDF: https://www.pt.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pt_pt/pdfs/toefl/speaking-rubrics.pdf
- ETS preparation resources and sample responses: https://www.ets.org/toefl/teachers-advisors-agents/ibt/teaching/preparing-students.html

## Product Rule

The app must say "estimated practice range" or equivalent wording. It must not
claim to produce an official IELTS or TOEFL score.

## IELTS Speaking Criteria

Use the official IELTS Speaking dimensions:

- Fluency and coherence.
- Lexical resource.
- Grammatical range and accuracy.
- Pronunciation.

If the app only has transcript text and no audio, pronunciation should be marked
as not assessed from text.

## TOEFL Speaking Criteria

Use ETS TOEFL Speaking rubric dimensions:

- Delivery.
- Language use.
- Topic development.

If the app only has transcript text and no audio, delivery features that require
audio should be marked as not assessed from text.

## Calibration Data Policy

For MVP, use internally written calibration examples rather than copying
third-party prediction-bank answers.

For production, import only:

- Official public sample responses where use is allowed.
- Licensed datasets.
- Internally written sample answers reviewed against official rubrics.
